# Motion by numbers

The method behind every film in this repo. A brief in eight lines with numbers in place of adjectives, every frame
worked out from time so any frame renders on its own, easing and springs as values, faults named by frame number and
fixed three at a time, and sound made in code from the same timings as the picture.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) before building any motion graphic. `render.sh` renders a composition with HyperFrames and
cuts the pictures you check it with.

Built on Florence's (@flxrnc) article on motion design with Claude and rari's (@0xwhrrari) "Motion Engineering",
plus what our own films taught us. Rendered with HyperFrames, HeyGen's open source engine. No key and no cost.
