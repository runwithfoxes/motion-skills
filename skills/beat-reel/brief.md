# The worked example's brief, in eight lines

The 95:5 rule in fifteen seconds. Write yours in the same shape before you touch build.py.

    GOAL: a marketer gets 95:5 in fifteen seconds: hardly anyone is buying now, so be on the list before they are.
    OUTPUT: one page, HyperFrames 0.8.143, 1080 x 1080.
    TIME: 15 s x 30 fps = 450 frames. 120 BPM, one beat = 15 frames, 30 beats. No loop.
    LOOK: deep #1A3A4E ground, off-white #FAFAF8 type, one accent light sky #6CAAC8 for the dots in the market,
          dots out of it at 16 percent white. Space Grotesk 500, JetBrains Mono for labels and the logo.
    MOTION: arrivals ease-out expo (0.16,1,0.3,1), exits ease-in and half as long, dot pops a spring at 0.55,
          counters linear. Cuts and hits on beats only.
    SHOTS: b0-b6 the field of 100 dots fills a row each half beat under "Everyone who could buy from you."
           b4.5-b6.5 five dots light one per half beat, "5%" lands with the fifth: "in a position to buy."
           b7-b10 the five pulse on every beat while the line is read.
           b11 the 5 rolls to 95 in one beat while a wave runs out through the grey dots: "no immediate need today."
           b16-b23 Q1 to Q4 every 1.5 beats, a different five lit each time: "A different small group each quarter."
           b23 the last line. b24 the logo and the source. Hold to b30.
    BANNED: gradients, glow, particles for decoration, everything fading in at once, more than one accent colour,
          text moving while it is read, a blank frame 0.
    QA: stills at b0, b6.5, b12, b17.5, b23.5, b29; a sheet; a dense scrub of b6 and b11; then the full render.

The words are from Paul Dervan's essay "The 95:5 rule: the Day One list". The source on screen: 95:5, John Dawes,
Ehrenberg-Bass Institute.
